## Byte Knight / Firewall

After charging and solving Firewall's prompt, grant Byte 12 shield points.

## Patch / Hotfix

Activate Hotfix to restore up to 8 HP to injured living allies. Unused healing does not become a shield.

## Query Mage / Index Mark

Activate Index Mark to pinpoint a weakness and deal 4 damage.

## Echo / Echo Shot

Activate Echo Shot to deal 4 follow-up damage.

## Fern Scout / Trail Guard

Activate Trail Guard to reduce the next single-target enemy attack by 6. Guards do not stack.

## Bolt / Static Wall

Activate Static Wall to grant Bolt 8 shield points.

## Pip / Packet Courier

Activate Packet Courier to grant 8 shield points to the living ally with the least shielding.

## Rune / Null Ward

Activate Null Ward to reduce the next AoE attack by 4 for every living ally. Guards do not stack.

## Nova / Nova Burst

Activate Nova Burst to deal 6 damage in a concentrated burst.

## Atlas / Load Balancer

Activate Load Balancer to reduce the next AoE attack by 8 for every other living ally.

## Zero / Kill Switch

Activate Kill Switch when the enemy has 80 HP or less to deal 10 damage. Skills cannot deliver the final blow before lesson completion.

## Sol / Dawn Protocol

Activate Dawn Protocol to grant 16 healing/shielding points. The first activation with a fallen ally also revives one ally at 35% max HP, once per stage attempt. Sol must be alive.

Skills are manual: each living character earns 8 bits from each lesson auto-attack. Eight bits equal one Byte. Costs vary by character from 12 to 24 bits and decrease by 1 bit per ascension star (minimum 8). A full bar highlights the portrait; click it and solve a character-themed prompt in the active campaign language. Run Code is safe practice; Use Skill spends charge only after a correct answer. Incorrect committed answers trigger normal retaliation but preserve charge. Unsupported forms and runtime-loading failures cause no retaliation.

Skill prompts never advance lesson objectives, award shards, generate charge or deliver a finishing blow. Normal lesson turns retain 20-25 basic damage, 40 spell damage and 40 utility without requiring skills. Skill charge and guards reset on stage entry or free retry. Guard effects are consumed by the next matching enemy attack; only living characters can activate skills. Sol's revival remains once per attempt.
